Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Summary

This site offers free fee and profit calculators for online sellers and freelancers. You do not need an account. The numbers you type into a calculator are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. The site is supported by third-party advertising, which may use cookies or similar technologies.

Information you enter

Prices, costs, shipping amounts, and other values you type into a calculator are used only to show you a result on your own device. If you upload a CSV file to the bulk comparison tool, the file is read and calculated locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to us.

Pro license and payments

Some features require a one-time Pro purchase. Payments are handled by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, and we do not receive or store your card details. When you enter a license key and press Activate, the key is sent to Lemon Squeezy to check that it is valid. If it is valid, your browser saves a small flag in its local storage so the Pro features stay unlocked on that device. You can remove this flag by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising

We show ads provided by a third-party advertising network (Adsterra). Ad networks may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and the pages you view, and may use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads. We do not control the data these companies collect. Please read the privacy policy of the advertising network for details about how they handle data.

You can limit ad personalization through your browser's cookie settings, or use industry opt-out tools such as optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.

Hosting and technical logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosting services, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not run our own analytics or user-tracking scripts. We use Google Search Console to see how the site performs in Google Search; it does not place tracking on your visit.

Cookies and local storage

The site itself stores only the Pro flag described above. Advertising partners may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the calculators will still work.

Estimates, not advice

Marketplace and payment fees change often. The calculators give estimates based on published fee rates, which may be out of date or may not cover every fee that applies to you. Always confirm the current fees on the platform's official website before making pricing or business decisions. Nothing on this site is financial, tax, or legal advice.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves beyond what is described above, most requests are best directed to the advertising and payment providers named in this policy. You can also contact us using the email below.

Changes to this policy
